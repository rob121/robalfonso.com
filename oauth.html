Google OAuth app information

OAuth Usage

This app uses Google OAuth to facilitate logging in with a Google account. The purpose of the OAuth connection is authentication: allowing a user to confirm their identity and access the app.

Why This Page Exists

Google requires apps that use OAuth to use authorized domains and to provide information that may be shown to users on the OAuth consent screen. This page explains the app's purpose and how Google OAuth is used.

Data Use

Google OAuth is used only for sign-in. The app requests access only to the information needed to authenticate the user, such as basic account identity information provided by Google during the sign-in flow.

User Control

Users can choose whether to authorize the app during Google's consent flow. Users may also revoke access later through their Google Account permissions.
